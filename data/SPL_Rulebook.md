## C. The Commissioner and Rule Changes

1. For rule changes, a majority (seven (7) votes) of the league is needed for a proposal to pass and change a rule.
2. In-season, if a matter is unclear in the existing rules or a rule needs changing because of a problem, the Commissioner has the power to bring the matter up for a league vote. In-season rule changes put to a vote require a majority.
3. In-season, special circumstances may require the Commissioner to make a judgement call on an ambiguous issue without a league vote. Such decisions are final.

## D. Trades and Trade Challenges
> status: confirm -- The trade vote runs in SPL HQ: 6 vetoes kills a trade, as written here.

1. During the Season, trades must be completed via the league software. Completed trades are subject to a 24-hour review by the SPL owners. Owners may veto a trade if 60% of the uninvolved teams vote to veto it. (If two teams complete a trade, it passes unless six (6) owners vote to veto.)
2. During the Off-Season, trades must be announced and accepted via email to the entire league. Off-Season trades are final unless an owner challenges within 72 hours. If an owner challenges, owners vote on it. Six (6) veto votes are required to veto the trade.

## E. Divisions and Divisional Draft

1. There will be four (4) divisions of three (3) teams each.
2. Divisions are selected each Off-Season during the **Divisional Draft**.
3. Draft order for the Divisional Draft:
   - Best record to miss the playoffs.
   - Second best record to miss the playoffs.
   - Wildcard loser on the side of the bracket without the SPL Champ.
   - Wildcard loser on the side of the bracket with the SPL Champ.
4. Divisional Draft rules:
   - Snake draft. The team who goes fourth also goes fifth.
   - Rather than selecting an owner to join one's own division, one may assign an owner to another owner's division. A given owner may only have up to one team assigned to his division this way.

## F. Slot Draft

1. The Slot Draft sets the draft order for the SPL Draft.
2. Order of selection:
   - **Choices 1–6:** inverse order of standings for non-playoff teams. The best team to miss the playoffs chooses #1, the second best #2, and so on.
   - **Choices 7–12:** final standings order. The SPL Champ chooses #12. The runner-up #11. The owner who lost to the Champ in Week 15 chooses #10. The owner who lost to the runner-up #9. The Wildcard Round loser on the Champ's side of the bracket #8. The Wildcard Round loser on the runner-up's side #7.

## G. Playoffs
> status: confirm -- Joe to confirm the seeding tiebreaks and the Seed 3 / Seed 1 picks for 2027.

1. Seeding for playoff spots 1–5 follows the **Tie Breaker Rules**, in order:
   - first by winning percentage,
   - then by points scored,
   - then by head-to-head record,
   - then by coin flip.
2. The #6 seed (the second wildcard) is the team with the most points scored after excluding the first five seeds; then head-to-head, then coin flip.
3. **Format.** Six (6) teams make the playoffs: four (4) division winners (seeds 1–4) and two (2) wildcards (seeds 5–6).
   - **First Round (Week 15):** Seeds 1 and 2 get byes. Seed 3 chooses its opponent from Seeds 4, 5 or 6; the other two play each other. The two winners advance.
   - **Second Round (Week 16):** Seed 1 chooses its opponent. The two remaining teams play each other. The two winners advance to the SPL Bowl.
   - **Third Round (Week 17):** SPL Bowl!
4. **Playoff tie breaker.** There are no ties in the playoffs. A tied score is first checked for fractional points. If that doesn't break it, the Tie Breaker Rules above apply, using standings and points as of the end of the Regular Season.

## J. Keeper Rules
> status: current

1. On **Keeper Signing Day**, each team gets 10 **Keeper Points** (KP).
2. KP may not be traded.
3. On Keeper Signing Day, each owner may carry at most three keepers (the **Three Keeper Max**). The max applies only on Keeper Signing Day.
4. All new keeper contracts are signed on Keeper Signing Day. Contracts are 1, 2 or 3 years; the term is fixed and announced at signing.
5. Cost to sign a keeper (Year One):

| Round drafted in the previous SPL Draft | Year One cost (KP) |
|---|---|
| First | 10 |
| Second | 8 |
| Third | 6 |
| Fourth or Fifth | 4 |
| Sixth, Seventh or Eighth | 3 |
| Ninth or later | 2 |
| Undrafted free agent | 2 |

6. Year Two cost = Year One + 1 KP. Year Three cost = Year Two + 1 KP.
7. **No extensions!** When a contract expires, that player can't be kept and returns to the pool for the next SPL Draft.
8. **Cut contracts.** If a keeper is dropped, the contract is immediately void and all penalties apply. If anyone picks the player up, the contract stays void and the penalties stay with the owner who cut him. The player is ineligible for a keeper contract at the end of the season.
9. If a keeper under contract is on no roster at the end of the Season, the owner who most recently rostered him is responsible for the contract. That **Unrostered Keeper** can't be retained; the contract is terminated.
10. **Contract termination** happens two ways: (i) an Unrostered Keeper, above; (ii) on Keeper Signing Day, the owner holding the keeper elects to terminate.
11. A player whose contract was terminated isn't eligible for a contract on that Signing Day and enters the upcoming SPL Draft. Whoever then drafts or adds him takes on none of the old Cap Penalty; he's a normal player, eligible for a new contract the next Off-Season.
12. **Cap Penalty.** A termination costs 0.5 × the KP scheduled for each remaining year of the contract. (Example: a 2 KP keeper on a three-year deal, terminated at the next Signing Day, costs 1.5 KP in Year Two and 2 KP in Year Three.)
13. Cap Penalties come off the owner's 10 KP before he spends any on current or new contracts.
14. Cap Penalty liability may be traded, whole, not in part. Cap Penalties and traded terminated contracts don't count toward the Three Keeper Max.
15. **The Franchise Tag.** A special 1, 2 or 3-year contract, signed on Keeper Signing Day, that counts toward the Three Keeper Max. Its cost: (i) normal KP in Year One, and (ii) the owner's original first-round pick in the upcoming SPL Draft. The KP is only owed in Year One, but the first-round pick is lost **every** year of the tag. An owner holding a Franchise Tag keeper may not trade his first-round pick.

## K. SPL Shoot Out

1. Week 14 carries a special prize for the week's highest score (see Dues and Prizes), on top of the weekly high-score prize.
2. After the Week 13 games, before Week 14, the league holds the **SPL Shoot Out Draft** to set Week 14 opponents.
3. Week 14 results count toward the final regular-season standings.
4. See Appendix Three for the Shoot Out Draft order and rules.

## Appendix Three · SPL Shoot Out Draft
> status: current

The Shoot Out Draft happens after Week 13 and before Week 14, and picks Week 14 opponents. Week 14 counts toward the final regular-season standings.

1. **If a matchup would directly decide a playoff berth, it gets made.**
   - Division winners come first. Two teams in the same division within 1 game **and** 50 points of each other play each other. If all three teams in a division are within 1 game, the top two (by league standings rules) play; the third plays the worst unmatched team currently in the playoffs.
   - Then the best-record Wildcard (once division matchups are set). Two teams within 1 game **and** 50 points play each other. Three teams within 1 game: the top two play, the third plays the worst unmatched team currently in the playoffs. Four teams within 1 game: first plays fourth, second plays third.
2. **The rest are drafted** in league-standings order at the end of Week 13: the top seed chooses an opponent from the unmatched teams, then the second seed, and so on. Teams already matched under Rule 1 can't choose or be chosen; skip to the next team.
